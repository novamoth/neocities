Brain Problem Games is a curated list of pieces of media that feature themes of:

  • Mental Health
  • Neurological Disorders
  • PTSD, CPTSD and Trauma
  • Dissociation
  • Identity

The inspiration was someone who commented that any list like this should be open to modification, and so I decided to make it an open github repository free for others to make PRs against. The list is deliberately all-inclusive, regardless of quality of the representation or problematic elements. However, I've stopped updating the list entirely, these days, since writing thoughtful reviews proved to be mentally taxing to do regularly.
