Brain Problem Games

Brain Problem Games was a curated list of pieces of media that feature themes of:

  • Mental Health
  • Neurological Disorders
  • PTSD, CPTSD and Trauma
  • Dissociation
  • Identity

Someone I was talking to commented that any list like this should be open to modification by the public, like a wiki, and so I decided to make it an open github repository free for others to make PRs against. This was the draw and also the fatal flaw, as it proved to be impossible to actually get people to submit thoughtful articles, and we were uncomfortable acting as the "arbiter of truth" of what characters were ill enough. Still, we've left the website up in perpetual stasis, with source code available to anyone who wishes to emulate it.
