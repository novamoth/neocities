From The Desk Of Iris Halloran

A 15k word twine game that sees you collect the lost pages of Iris Halloran's journal, a woman who Is Not Real.

A fan game based on our Fallen London character's personal arc, and the way she moved from a prevalent writer to an inflamatory revolutionary to Nothing At All.

Once, she wanted to be known by all. What secrets would be worth giving up on that dream? What secrets would be worth becoming Unknown By All?

Can any measure of pain or punishment stop her from holding her soul over a candle until it is nothing but cinders and ash?

You know the answers to these questions.

This story riffs on the Seeking Mr. Eaten's Name questline, which involves a mix of ARG secret-hunting and deliberate self-harm of your character. At its ultimate culmination, your character--often the culmination of real-life years of work--is permanently and irrevocably Gone.

It started with a more modest scope and less personal weight. But when I broke it off with my bastard ex of twelve wasted years, I couldn't write a normal work of fanfiction. Especially not one whose source material is based in a story of self-harm. A scene that was once an in-game reputation loss became an interactive game representing social ostricization. A scene of fantastical self-harm became more horrifically grounded.

For these reasons, we do not share this project often. Sharing it is a sign of trust. But in either case, it is our most significant work; It is my most significant work.
