HELPING MY GM FRIEND <3

I've been the "VFX Moth" on a friend's FoundryVTT campaign for a while now!!! When she needs some form of audiovisual or technical effect to bring her vision to life, I've helped make sure it is possible!! She's REALLY good, like super professional quality, so like, I wanna make sure she can. This included a TON of stuff like creating Sequencer macros so bosses can have special unique attacks, finding new add-ons for her to use, and even creating custom-programmed Modules for her (to display an area's title).

But by faaaar the most complex thing I did was the Avarice Act 1 Recap. She recorded a series of voice lines that she wanted edited, but I took it upon myself to make an entire lightly-animated video. Like, tokens moving back and forth to mimic what happened. This came out to final runtime of 17 minutes, and was really complicated because it used stuff like geometry nodes and custom video filters.

Stupidest thing I ever did, in like, the best way.
