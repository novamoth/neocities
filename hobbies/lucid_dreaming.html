Lucid Dreaming

When most people lay their weary heads upon the pillow, their brain conjures for them a wonderland of missed tests, forgotten belts, and rotting teeth. An amalgam of a dozen details from their personal life, that they are convinced is reality.

But for us, it is not so. We can feel the body growing numb and paralyzed, and feel ourselves slip within. We know we are asleep, and can confront our dreams as more than an unknowledgeable observer.

This is a practiced and cultivated skill. We record our dreams in a journal before they might fade, which acts as a command to the subconscious that these memories are important and not to be carelessly abandoned. We practice slipping asleep deliberately rather than waiting for it to take us.

Different alters have differing skills. Rogue is nearly never aware it is asleep, for example. Aster, on the contrary, is almost always aware when it is asleep, and it manipulates the dream to its whims.

The most skilled of us all, however, is me. That is why I am the Princess Alpheratz von Andromedae--for the conjured kingdom that I can slip into whenever I close my eyes. For the castle and kingdom I created within my dreams, weaving them into being.

These days, however, I don't visit often. I delight, instead, in weaving new things, for those less skilled. Creating scenarios for the others in our head to adventure within. Perhaps I am a storyteller--perhaps, I am playing with dolls.

This has shaped large portions of our life, and driven both escapism and inspiration. Our dreams are vivid and beautiful--how could it not shape how we see reality?
