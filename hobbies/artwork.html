We have long since had a deep desire to learn the visual arts, but had to slowly work up to it, as we hadn't truly started prior to last year.

Aster has made silly edits to photos in photoshop (shrinking that one man's face (you know the one!) being a favorite), but aside from that, we had no experience with visual arts of any kind. We started to learn 3D modeling, which we adored, but only recently hit a particular stride with proper tablet-canvas artwork.

I, myself, adore digital painting. I pretend as though control-Z no longer exists, and brush in rhythmic strokes.

Medium, elsewise, tends to overuse control-Z and is a stickler for details--often to the detriment of actually finishing the work, I will confess!

Overall, we are still extremely novice, but passionately working to improve.
